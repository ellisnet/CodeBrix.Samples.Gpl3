=== MIDI Settings ===

Here you can configure {appname}'s MIDI settings.

{appname} plays MIDI itself, with a software synthesizer built into the
program. There is nothing to connect and no MIDI port to choose: a synthesizer
does not have to be installed, started or wired up, and playback works the same
way on every platform.

= Instrument =

The sound the synthesizer plays with comes from a SoundFont — a file of
recorded instruments. {appname} comes with a General MIDI SoundFont and uses it
unless you choose another one. Press *Change...* to pick a `.sf2` or `.sfz`
file of your own, and *Default* to go back to the one that came with the
program.

= Preferences =

*Volume* sets how loud playback is.
