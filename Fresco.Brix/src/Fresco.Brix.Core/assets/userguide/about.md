=== About Fresco.Brix ===

{appname} is a music score editor for LilyPond source files. It engraves your
scores itself, in its own process, and needs no LilyPond installation.

{appname} is modelled on
[https://www.frescobaldi.org/ Frescobaldi], the LilyPond music editor by
Wilbert Berendsen and contributors, and is deeply indebted to it. It is not
Frescobaldi, and neither the Frescobaldi project nor its authors endorse it.
Frescobaldi in turn is named after
[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girolamo_Frescobaldi
Girolamo Frescobaldi (1583-1643)], an Italian organist and composer.
